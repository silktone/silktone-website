Bio

Joel Thomas Whitley

Joel Thomas Whitley (Silktone) is a multi-instrumentalist from San Diego, California, based in Los Angeles: guitarist, bassist, songwriter and producer. Guitar and bass are his first languages; writing and production are where he puts them to work.

He has toured with Stevie Wonder, Lauryn Hill, Natalie Cole, Thundercat, Everlast, House of Pain, Ebi, James Ingram, Ledisi, Mary Mary, Tonéx, Andraé Crouch, and Marsha Ambrosius. In the studio he has recorded and collaborated with Dr. Dre, Eminem, Prince, The Temptations, and Raphael Saadiq, among others, with guitar on Kamasi Washington’s Fearless Movement (2024), bass and guitar on Carly Rae Jepsen’s Dedicated Side B (2020), bass and co-writing on Eric Gales’ Good for Sumthin’ (2014), guitar, bass and co-writing on Everlast’s Songs of the Ungrateful Living (2011), and writer on Eminem’s “Ridaz” from Recovery (2010).

His sound lives where R&B, soul, funk, jazz, and pop meet, and his playing serves the song first. Alongside sessions and touring, he produces through his own company, Sounds Good Ent. LLC, builds his own instruments and software, and teaches guitarists the feel behind the notes.
